How Cycling Relieves Stress

Cycling is one of the most effective ways to clear your mind and boost your mental well-being. Getting on a bicycle forces you to focus on balance, rhythm, and your immediate surroundings, which naturally pulls your thoughts away from daily worries.

During a steady ride, your body releases mood-lifting endorphins while lowering cortisol levels. Regular aerobic exercise also promotes better sleep and mental clarity, a concept well-documented by the Wikipedia cycling guide and various health studies.
